# Shivam Kumar — Portfolio Creative Direction

**Working title:** Model to Motion  
**Status:** Implemented in the static portfolio.  
**Audience:** AI engineering, machine-learning, and software-development recruiters and collaborators.

## Direction

The portfolio follows an idea through the path from input, to model, to something a person can use. The visual language pairs warm editorial paper with ink-dark technical canvases, coral signal marks, and teal data paths. It should feel curious and engineered, while keeping Shivam’s real work and real photographs at the center.

The Razorpay references point toward a useful content principle: make the journey from a real problem to a built agent or system easy to follow. The accessible [Razorpay Careers page](https://razorpay.com/careers/) describes AI systems built across design, product, and engineering; the indexed [Razorpay Builders Day page](https://razorpay.com/m/builders-day/) is organized around identifying a problem, building an agent, and shipping it. The portfolio applies that problem-to-build-to-impact rhythm to the internship and project stories without copying Razorpay’s branding.

The seven Pinterest short links were supplied as visual references, but they did not resolve in the available reference viewer. Their specific imagery, layouts, colors, and type cannot be claimed as inspected. They remain recorded below so they can be revisited if direct pin pages or screenshots become available.

## Visual system

| Role | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Warm paper | `#F3F0E7` | Main reading canvas |
| Raised paper | `#FFFDF7` | Cards and image borders |
| Machine ink | `#132425` | Body text, project canvases, experience panel |
| Coral signal | `#E96848` | Primary actions, key figures, focal emphasis |
| Teal data | `#168B86` | Model connections, secondary emphasis, interest labels |
| Structural line | `#D3D7CE` | Dividers, card edges, quiet diagrams |

- **Display:** Syne for expressive, compact headlines.
- **Body:** Manrope for readable paragraphs and interface labels.
- **Annotations:** IBM Plex Mono for dates, indices, and technical metadata.
- **Layout:** Editorial asymmetry, generous margins, a large human portrait, and dark schematic panels for projects. The page stays readable as ordinary HTML; decoration never carries essential information.
- **Motion:** Short reveals and restrained orbit or diagram movement support the story. Reduced-motion preferences remain respected.

## Page narrative

1. **Hero:** “I build AI that moves from model to motion.” The portrait, AI/ML and software focus, project route, résumé, and contact route appear immediately.
2. **The builder:** A concise explanation of interests in LLMs, transformers, machine learning, agents, and software systems, paired with a genuine event photo.
3. **Experience and practice:** Bluestock Fintech is the central work story. Role, dates, tools, records processed, and résumé-reported workflow outcomes are separated visually for easy scanning. Education is intentionally left to the résumé.
4. **Project worlds:** AgentHub, Newsify, CortexCare, and the Multiple Disease Prediction System retain their conceptual illustrations and source links. The diagrams are labeled as concepts, not product screenshots. The résumé’s Multiple Disease Prediction demo remains the only live demo link provided.
5. **Learning and credentials:** J.P. Morgan Quantitative Research job simulation, Oracle GenAI and AI Foundations credentials, and AWS Cloud Essentials. The simulation is identified as a simulation, not employment.
6. **Contact:** Direct email, LinkedIn, LeetCode, GitHub, and résumé actions.

## Photography and content integrity

- `assets/shivam-portrait.webp` is the supplied seated portrait with a natural crop and light tonal correction. It appears in the hero.
- `assets/shivam-event.webp` is the supplied event photograph, lightly cropped and color-corrected without generative edits. It replaces the abstract “curiosity” graphic in the About section.
- The Gemini-generated portrait is not used. Its generated appearance and visible watermark conflict with the request to keep the face authentic.
- No face shape, features, skin texture, expression, or identity have been changed.
- Internship and project performance figures are transcribed from the supplied résumé and labeled as reported. No medical or clinical validation is implied for CortexCare or the prediction project.
- Project repositories and the résumé’s live-demo URL are the supplied destinations. Project implementation details were not independently verified during this redesign.
